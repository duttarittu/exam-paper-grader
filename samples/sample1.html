Q3. Explain why we see a rainbow after rain. [6 marks]
When sun light go through small water drops in the air, the light
bend and split into different colour. This make a rainbow shape
in the sky. It happen only when sun is behind us and rain drop
infront.
Q4. Describe the water cycle. [10 marks]
The water cycle has three main steps. First, evaporation - heat
from the sun turns water from oceans, rivers and lakes into
water vapour which rises into the air. Second, condensation -
as the vapour rises it cools down and changes back into tiny
water droplets, forming clouds. Third, precipitation - when the
droplets in the clouds become heavy enough, they fall back to
the earth as rain, snow or hail. The water that falls collects in
rivers, lakes and oceans again, and the whole cycle repeats.
This cycle keeps fresh water moving around the planet.
